About Us

Syrian Bridges of return is a non-profit organization dedicated to supporting vulnerable communities through social, educational, and health initiatives. We believe in spreading kindness and hope to those who need it most.

Our Programs

Education

We provide educational opportunities for Syrian children and youth through schools and training courses.

Relief

We support families with food baskets, medical care, and emergency assistance during crises.

Empowerment

We empower women and youth through vocational training and small business initiatives.

Our Projects

Food Distribution

Providing essential food packages to families in need, ensuring no one sleeps hungry.

Education for All

Offering scholarships, school supplies, and programs to empower children through education.

Healthcare Support

Delivering medical aid, medicines, and health awareness campaigns to save lives.
