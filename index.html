About Us

Bridges of Return is a non-profit humanitarian organization dedicated to restoring hope through education, relief, and empowerment for individuals inside and outside Syria.

Our Programs

Education

We provide educational opportunities for Syrian children and youth through schools and training courses.

Relief

We support families with food baskets, medical care, and emergency assistance during crises.

Empowerment

We empower women and youth through vocational training and small business initiatives.

Our Projects

Rebuilding schools in northern Syria for displaced children.

Launching health campaigns in refugee camps.

Supporting women's entrepreneurship in Aleppo and Idlib.
